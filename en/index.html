Taxes and bookkeeping

Receipts, transactions and invoices in one place. From them the app computes the income-surplus statement (EÜR), the VAT returns, trade tax and the separate assessment for partnerships, and files them through ELSTER.

It runs on your machine: your bank data and documents stay in a local store. Every figure can be traced back to the transaction it came from.

AI is optional: everything works without it. If you want help from an AI assistant, switch on the built-in MCP server and connect the assistant of your choice. It can read your books and, only if you allow it, make changes.

The tax logic follows German law, so the interface keeps the official German terms. The app is a preparation aid, not tax advice.

Changes in version 0.1.2

The AI assistant is optional and can be connected through the built-in MCP server — every provider supported by Lotse can be used, and free hosted models stay refused.
